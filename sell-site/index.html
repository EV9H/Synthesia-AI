FAQ

Straight answers. No vaporware claims.

The preview piano sounds cheap. Did I buy a synth?

No. Preview audio is a sketch so you can hear the MIDI shape. The product is the notes — drag them onto your piano, bass, and drums. A thin built-in piano is expected, not a defect.

Is this an AI that listens to audio?

No. Harmonia is a MIDI generator. It plans and writes notes. You hear them through whatever instruments you load in your DAW. Preview MP3s on this page are illustrative renders only.

What do I get after purchase?

A download link and a license key HRM-XXXXXXXX-CCCC for the VST3 (macOS / Windows). Settings → paste the key → Validate. Generate needs the key on paid builds. Drag MIDI and export always work, even before you paste it.

Generate is greyed out. Is it broken?

No — paid builds lock Generate until the key is pasted (Settings). The first-run tour has an Open Settings button. Drag MIDI still works so you can audition a clip that is already on the piano roll.

macOS / Windows says the plugin is unsafe

Unsigned builds trip Gatekeeper and SmartScreen. That warning is expected until the developer signs the installer — it is not malware. Prefer the signed download from your receipt once it exists.

Do I need an internet connection?

Text-to-arrangement and Inspire use a cloud LLM for the plan. Genre templates and MIDI editing work offline once generated. Details ship with the installer notes.

Which DAWs?

Any host that loads VST3 and accepts dragged MIDI (Ableton Live, FL Studio, Logic via wrapper / AU roadmap, Reaper, Cubase, Studio One, and similar). Always test the trial build in your host before buying when available.

Can I use the MIDI commercially?

Yes — MIDI you generate with a valid license is yours to use in commercial releases. Do not redistribute the plugin itself.

Is this related to Synthesia?

No. The product is Harmonia, an AI MIDI generator for your DAW. It is not affiliated with Synthesia Learning Co. or any piano-learning software of that name.
