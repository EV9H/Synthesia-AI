What Harmonia is

Harmonia is a VST3 MIDI generator that runs on your computer. The product is arrangement MIDI you drag into a DAW. Preview audio is a local sketch, not a cloud render we store.

What we do not collect in the plugin

The plugin does not create a Harmonia account. License keys, optional LLM API keys, and Settings URLs stay in local plugin state on this machine (SynthesiaAI/settings.xml). We do not receive your MIDI, stems, or project files.

Optional cloud calls you turn on

Text-to-arrangement and Inspire send a short prompt to the LLM provider you configure (OpenAI, Groq, or a custom endpoint). That traffic goes to that provider under their terms, not to a Harmonia server. Genre templates work offline.

Payments and licenses

Checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy (merchant of record). They process payment, tax, receipts, and (when enabled) license keys. Their privacy policy applies to that purchase. If you paste a Lemon key in Settings, the plugin may POST that key to the validate URL you set (HARMONIA_LICENSE_VALIDATE_URL or Settings → Advanced). Offline HRM- keys are checked on-device and are never sent anywhere.

This website

The marketing site is static. It does not set an account cookie. Your host (for example Cloudflare Pages) may log standard request metadata. Demo MP3s are illustrative preview audio, not buyer projects.
