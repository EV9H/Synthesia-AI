Harmonia — license note (this zip)

Shipping brand: Harmonia. The VST3 bundle may still be named "Synthesia AI"
until a dedicated rename. Same plugin.

What you bought
  Arrangement MIDI for your DAW. Preview audio is a cheap sketch.

Activate
  Settings → paste the key from your receipt → Validate (or tap Paste).
  Offline format: HRM-XXXXXXXX-CCCC
  Lemon keys (if your receipt has one) validate over HTTPS when
  HARMONIA_LICENSE_VALIDATE_URL is set. Offline HRM- keys never leave this machine.

What stays unlocked without a key
  Drag MIDI and export. Generate locks only on paid (ENFORCE) builds.
  Tap Generate — Settings opens if the key is missing.

Do not
  Redistribute this zip or share your key.
  Expect a synth or sample pack. Load YOUR piano, bass, and drums.

Unsigned builds
  See UNSIGNED_SMOKE.txt. Gatekeeper / SmartScreen warnings are expected
  until the developer signs the installer.
