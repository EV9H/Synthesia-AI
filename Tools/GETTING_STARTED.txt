Harmonia — getting started (30 seconds)

Harmonia is a MIDI generator, not a synth. The product is the notes.
Preview audio is a cheap sketch so you can hear the shape.

1. Insert Harmonia on a MIDI / instrument track in Ableton, Logic, or FL.
2. Pick a genre (or an Inspire seed) and tap Generate.
3. Drag MIDI from the handle into your DAW.
4. Load YOUR piano, bass, and drums on those tracks.

Unsigned build: macOS Gatekeeper and Windows SmartScreen will warn.
That is expected until the developer signs the installer. The zip is not malware.

License: paste an HRM-XXXXXXXX-CCCC key in Settings. Drag and export stay
unlocked even before a key. Generate locks only on paid (ENFORCE) builds.
