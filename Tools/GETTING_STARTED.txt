Harmonia — getting started (30 seconds)

Harmonia is a MIDI generator, not a synth. The product is the notes.
Preview audio is a cheap sketch so you can hear the shape. If the
preview piano sounds thin, that is expected — load your own sounds.

1. Insert Harmonia on a MIDI / instrument track in Ableton, Logic, or FL.
2. Pick a genre (or an Inspire seed — the combo generates immediately).
3. Tap Generate. You get five tracks: Chords, Bass, Synth, Piano, Drums.
4. Drag the handle labelled "Drag MIDI" into your DAW (not the piano-roll).
5. Load YOUR piano, bass, and drums on those tracks.

If Generate is greyed out: Settings → paste HRM-XXXXXXXX-CCCC → Validate.
Drag and export stay unlocked even before a key. Generate locks only on
paid (ENFORCE) builds.

Unsigned build: macOS Gatekeeper and Windows SmartScreen will warn.
That is expected until the developer signs the installer. The zip is not malware.
